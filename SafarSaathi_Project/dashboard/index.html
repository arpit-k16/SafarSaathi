What this system does NOT claim

  • No disaster forecast. "Hazard" means a modelled day of at least 64.5 mm of rain, not a flood, landslide or earthquake.
  • No flood extent from any source. No documented flood mask was obtainable on this host, so none is used or simulated.
  • No DEM. Copernicus DEM pixels do not decode here, so there is no slope, runoff or terrain term anywhere in the system.
  • No radar. Sentinel-1 does not decode here.
  • Review sentiment is not destination sentiment; the corpora contain no geography at all.
  • Travel times come from assumed speeds per road class, not measurement.
  • Accommodation counts are relative weights from an incomplete OSM extract, never absolute bed counts.
  • Scenarios are stress tests. None of them is a prediction of what will happen.
