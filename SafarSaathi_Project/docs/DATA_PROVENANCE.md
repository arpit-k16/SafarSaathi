# Data provenance and redistribution

This file describes the data actually used. Acquisition snapshots and checksums are recorded in `release/india_scope_20261025_03/source_manifests.json`; fuller source-access decisions are in `source_investigation.json` and `DATA_LICENSES_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md`.

| Data | Scope / target | Source and transformation | Redistribution / missingness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel reviews | Source-labeled binary positive/negative reviews | Existing local source dataset; persisted TF-IDF/logistic pipeline | Raw reviews are not included in release. Labels and source domain limitations apply. |
| Annual arrivals | India annual international tourist arrivals, 1995–2019 | World Bank WDI `ST.INT.ARVL`, existing locally collected snapshot | Public source attribution; raw source excluded. 25 annual values; no claim beyond 2019. |
| Monthly FTA | India monthly Foreign Tourist Arrivals, 2019-01–2024-12 | Tables in official India Tourism Data Compendium 2022–2025 PDFs, extracted/validated as 72 monthly counts | Original PDFs remain local and outside release. No imputed months; NRI arrivals excluded by definition. |
| Flood benchmark | STURM Sentinel-1 flood/water-scene reference masks | Zenodo record 12748983, locally approved 3,985,215,183-byte archive; bounded tile reads, event/country grouping, mask > 0 target | Raw benchmark archive excluded from release. Attribution and archive terms are recorded in `STURM_ATTRIBUTION.md`; only compact model artifact/evaluation is packaged. |
| Cyclone count | NI basin MAIN tropical-storm IDs, 1980–2024, monthly archive counts | NOAA NCEI IBTrACS v04r01 NI CSV; count each distinct SID once in first qualifying TS month; 540 months | NOAA redistribution metadata permits unrestricted use with attribution. Revised archive vintage; source CSV excluded. Zero means zero eligible archive SIDs only. |
| PM2.5 | India modeled annual population-weighted exposure, 1990–2023, µg/m³ | World Bank WDI `EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3`, based on GBD2023/IHME; no target imputation | WDI CC BY 4.0 attribution; raw snapshot excluded. It is modeled national exposure, not sensor or destination data. |
| Historical tourism/environment indicators | National annual 2011–2024 and 36 State/UT estimates/reports for 2023–2024; national environmental context through source-specific latest years | Official India Tourism Data Compendium tables 4.1.1/4.1.2 and World Bank/FAO indicators. State values retain rounded million units and source-revision/estimate flags. National PM2.5, water stress and surface area are not allocated to states. | Compact derived snapshot and provenance are included. Missing values remain null; no interpolation, invented capacity, composite stress score, or causal claim. Visits/km²/year is a surface-area intensity, not occupancy. |

World Bank surface area and water stress sources retain source indicator codes and CC BY 4.0 attribution. See source manifests for retrieval metadata/checksums. The release excludes raw source files, external archives, credentials and caches. Reproduction commands that require external inputs are in `INDIA_SCOPE_MODELS.md`; never substitute synthetic fixtures for training evidence.
