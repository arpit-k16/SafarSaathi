## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-stakes operations, executive governance, and mission-critical telemetry environments where precision, authority, and discretion are paramount. The aesthetic merges executive luxury with hyper-technical command interfaces: deep obsidian monoliths, finely milled metallic accents, and pristine optical clarity.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill an unshakeable sense of sovereignty, absolute tactical control, and refined craftsmanship. It feels whisper-quiet, dense yet scannable, and uncompromisingly high-end—analogous to bespoke instrumentation inside a luxury performance vehicle or an orbital mission control suite.

### Design Movement
**Technical Haute-Horlogerie & Glassmorphic Brutalism**:
- Deep layered obsidian foundations (`#080A0F`, `#0D1117`, `#161B22`) punctuated by razor-thin, metallic-sheened linear boundaries.
- Precise luminous metallic accents in pale gilt and radiant amber, calibrated to avoid garishness while commanding visual hierarchy.
- Micro-telemetry displays, tabular numeric alignments, and delicate optical backdrops that convey institutional rigor.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively around an absolute low-light baseline, ensuring optimal pupil stability during prolonged command operations while deploying precious-metal wavelengths for hierarchy and status.

### Base Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#080A0F`)**: Deepest void obsidian. Used for system-level root backgrounds and non-interactive troughs.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0D1117`)**: Primary container background for main instrumentation decks, sidebars, and analytical canvases.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#161B22`)**: Elevated card structures, operational cells, floating popovers, and segmented controls.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#21262D`)**: Interactive component rests, hovered containers, and nested micro-telemetry panels.

### Accent & Metallic Tokens
- **Primary Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: Focal commands, active metrics, primary KPIs, and prominent micro-borders. Represents primary authority.
- **Secondary Vivid Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Dynamic telemetry, critical transitions, high-priority status indicators, and energetic highlights.
- **Tertiary Pale Gold (`#EAB308`)**: Supporting telemetry badges, secondary trends, and active peripheral selections.
- **Light Amber Luster (`#FBBF24`)**: Peak luminescent glows, active cursor targets, and optical highlights.

### Functional & Monochromatic Spectrum
- **Text Primary (`#F0F6FC`)**: Near-white silver for definitive legibility on deep obsidian.
- **Text Secondary (`#8B949E`)**: Slate gray for axis markers, unit descriptors, and sub-labels.
- **Text Tertiary (`#484F58`)**: Subdued technical metadata, grid references, and inactive timestamps.
- **Borders & Seams (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`)**: Ultra-fine metallic wireframes that structure the interface without visual mass.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes distinct roles: **Space Grotesk** serves as the commanding, structural headline voice; **Hanken Grotesk** maintains neutral, low-strain legibility across narrative text; and **JetBrains Mono** governs dense operational telemetry, data grids, coordinates, and system timestamps.

### Typographic Rules
- All numeric readouts, analytical matrices, tabular tickers, and status labels must strictly use the monospace telemetry levels with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Label caps are rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to function as tactical category tags and section dividers.
- Maintain high-contrast ratios: never degrade primary telemetry below 7:1 against background obsidian containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a high-density, mathematical 12-column fluid grid system engineered for tactical operational displays, multi-pane telemetry, and expansive command dashboards.

### Grid Infrastructure
- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column fluid grid with `gutter-lg` (1.5rem) and `margin-lg` (2.5rem). Modules can be grouped into 3-, 4-, 6-, or 12-column analytical panels.
- **Tablet / Secondary Screens (768px – 1439px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1.5rem). Side rails collapse into pinned icon ribbons.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-sm` (0.5rem) and `margin-sm` (1rem). Telemetry streams stack vertically with sticky metric headers.

### Density and Rhythm
Interfaces maintain high informational density. The 4px baseline grid dictates interior card padding, micro-telemetry gaps, and aligned operational rows. Spacing tokens strictly resolve external distribution and internal content clearance:
- Use `space-xs` (0.25rem) for tight icon-to-metric pairings and badge tags.
- Use `space-sm` (0.5rem) between stacked telemetry rows and tabular lines.
- Use `space-md` (0.75rem) as the standard inner padding for command tiles and input fields.
- Use `space-lg` (1.25rem) for macro-grouping inside tactical modules.
- Use `space-xl` (2rem) for perimeter offsets and modal separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or bright surfaces. Instead, depth is articulated through layered obsidian strata, micro-milled gold linear outlines, and low-frequency amber photonic glows.

### Tonal Stratification
1. **Floor 0 (Canvas `#080A0F`)**: Lowest level; houses cosmic grid indicators and global structure dividers.
2. **Floor 1 (Decks `#0D1117`)**: Base cards, analytics viewports, and primary layout blocks. Defined by a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
3. **Floor 2 (Command Tiles `#161B22`)**: Interactive widgets, operational triggers, and data modules.
4. **Floor 3 (Overlays & Flyouts `#21262D`)**: Contextual inspect panes, dropdown menus, and tactical tooltips.

### Photonic Amber & Gold Glows
- **Resting Focus**: 1px perimeter border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Active State / Armed State**: 1px perimeter border of `#D4AF37`, accompanied by an outer luminescence: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.35)`.
- **Alert / Over-threshold State**: 1px perimeter border of `#F59E0B` with an ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.
- **Glass Transparency**: Modals and floating control heads employ a subtle backing blend: `background: rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.85)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on architectural precision, sharp cuts, and technical discipline. Roundedness is kept strictly at level `1` (Soft), yielding subtle 0.25rem (4px) structural corner radii.

### Geometric Rules
- **Standard Controls & Badges**: 4px border radius (`0.25rem`). This provides tactile cohesion without softening the uncompromising command aesthetic.
- **Cards, Frames, and Tactical Containers**: 6px border radius (`0.375rem` or `rounded-lg` equivalent).
- **Telemetry Indicators & Micro-Status Nodes**: 2px border radius or pure circular pips (for live state indicators only).
- **Chamfered / Technical Cuts**: Key high-tier command triggers and telemetry badge containers can selectively feature 45-degree chamfered corner cuts (3px corner clipping) via CSS clip-path to reinforce aerospace and precision-instrument associations.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Command**: Base background `#D4AF37` with typography in `#080A0F` (bold, JetBrains Mono or Space Grotesk). Top edge highlighted with a 1px inner line of `#FBBF24`. Hover transitions trigger a luminous aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Sub-system**: Base background `#161B22`, border of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`, typography in `#F0F6FC`. On hover, the border shifts to `#D4AF37` with typography warming to `#FBBF24`.
- **Destructive / Override**: Dark obsidian background with `1px solid #EF4444`, red text, emitting a sharp crimson diffuse field on engagement.

### Telemetry Badges & Chips
- **Status Badges**: Ultra-compact containers (`height: 20px`, padding: `0 6px`) using `telemetry-sm` uppercase. 
  - *Nominal*: Obsidian `#0D1117` base, gold border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, text in `#D4AF37`. Accompanied by a 4px pulsing gold dot.
  - *Active Telemetry*: `#161B22` base, vivid amber text `#F59E0B`, edge glow `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- **Filter Chips**: Dark-surfaced toggles (`#161B22`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Selected state activates an amber underline accent (2px) and gold text.

### Telemetry Data Cards & Modules
- Structured in `#0D1117` with a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- Card headers feature uppercase `label-caps` typography in `#8B949E` flanked by micro-telemetry coordinates (e.g., `SYS.LOC // 01-A`).
- KPI readouts use `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono` at `headline-lg` in `#F0F6FC`, accompanied by small upward/downward delta arrows tinted `#D4AF37` or `#F59E0B`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat background `#080A0F` set inside containers with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#484F58`. Focused state drives the border to `#D4AF37` and applies an interior gradient baseline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 14x14px technical squares (`rounded: 2px`) styled with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`. Checked state fills `#D4AF37` with an obsidian `#080A0F` checkmark.
- **Segmented Switch**: Obsidian trough (`#080A0F`) with sliding active pill in `#21262D`, demarcated by an amber-gold border and crisp white label.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers utilize `telemetry-sm` in `#8B949E` with bottom borders in `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- Rows alternate or rest on transparent rows with hover states revealing a subtle background wash of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.03)` and a left-aligned 2px gold highlight edge.
- Monospaced numeric alignments aligned strictly to the right.

### Command HUD HUD-Rail & Crosshairs
- Specialized command elements include corner crosshair markings (`+` glyphs at 50% gold opacity) on major viewport boundaries.
- Live data streaming widgets feature a 1px scanning gold-amber raster rule that softly animates across the telemetry timeline during active sync.
