# Case-study limitations

- Primary web records verify the event identity/date but are not a local numerical event dataset. Their contents can be revised; source review date and access failures are explicit in event_sources.json.
- The full ICIMOD PDF could not be fetched through its signed storage redirect, the official government attachment and candidate NDRRMA RDNA PDF timed out, and UNOSAT's page required JavaScript. Only directly read record/page content is used. These access limits are not evidence that the underlying reports do not exist.
- The original EMSR927 notice uses an early mechanism description; later scientific wording is more qualified. No definitive causal mechanism, rainfall forcing or earthquake causation is assigned by this study.
- The UNESCO synthesis contained conflicting aggregate mapped areas within one page. Those figures were excluded instead of being promoted to quantitative ground truth; original versioned mapping products and class definitions are needed.
- Local raw scenes declare Uttarakhand coverage, and STURM metadata are older/non-Nepal. Filename discovery cannot prove all local files lack Nepal content, but no event-ready aligned Nepal input/reference package was identified. Nepal annual national statistics, if present in all-country World Bank files, do not supply daily location-level exposure.
- A water/reference-positive model and a debris/mass-movement extent product can describe different targets. Alignment of coordinate systems alone would not make their comparison valid.
- Potential asset intersection is exposure, not confirmed damage; road intersection is not route accessibility; historical reconstruction is not advance prediction. No current emergency advice, evacuation route or operational alert follows from these results.
- Acquisition of bounded Nepal pre/event/post imagery, independent labeled references, dated Nepal infrastructure/road-state data and a tourist-exposure panel is future work. It must preserve source licenses, metadata, dates, independent holdouts and raw files. No enormous download or unreviewed model replacement was undertaken.

**Readiness:** event-source verification is supported; event-level scientific validation remains partial and data-blocked. Do not label the full historical case study or broader research validation complete.
