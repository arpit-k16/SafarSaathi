## Findings that change interpretation

The requested disaster is not unsupported merely because the initial project collection lacked its data. A benchmark-format inference response, a model checksum, or an illustrative map is software evidence; none measures this case's scientific accuracy.

No rainfall, water-level, death, damaged-asset, tourist or road-closure number was invented or added to the application. Different humanitarian or remote-mapping summaries were not reconciled by averaging incompatible vintages/areas/target classes.
