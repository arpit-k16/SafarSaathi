# Rasuwagadhi event verification

**Verified result:** the requested **26 August 2026** event is independently documented. Nepal's Ministry of Home Affairs records that date for flooding on the Bhote Koshi in Rasuwa. ICIMOD's later synthesis explicitly places the disaster in the Gyirong–Rasuwagadhi corridor. This is a Nepal/China border case, not an Uttarakhand event. [Government appeal](https://www.moha.gov.np/en/post/ha-ra-tha-ka-apa-l-11), [ICIMOD synthesis record](https://lib.icimod.org/records/qdvc1-tc123).

The government notice is dated 2083-05-11 and links a 27 August 2026 notice; its event date is the preceding day. ICIMOD's record displays publication **13 October 2026** and repository creation **14 October**. These are publication/repository dates, not alternative disaster dates.

## Evidence progression and uncertainty

The [same-day ICIMOD advisory](https://www.icimod.org/press-release/major-flash-flood-sweeps-through-nepals-rasuwa-district-raising-fears-of-further-downstream-flooding/) treated the proposed rock/ice trigger and temporary river blockage as questions under investigation. The October record describes a cascading cryospheric geohazard and warns that the initiating mechanism remains unresolved. No earthquake causation, measured rainfall forcing or conventional GLOF mechanism is inferred here.

The [Copernicus EMS notice](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/flood-in-nepal-emsr927/) independently dates activation EMSR927 to 26 August; its initial GLOF wording is provisional and must not override later mechanism evidence. [UN Nepal's OCHA/RCO index](https://nepal.un.org/en/323255-nepal-rasuwa-floods-2026-situation-reports) lists the same-day flash update and subsequent reports through 12 October.

## Verification scope

Event existence, calendar date and named corridor are supported by directly opened primary web records as of **30 October 2026**. This is source verification, not a reconstruction of observed hydrographs, rainfall, deaths, damage or tourist exposure. No such numbers are added to SafarSaathi. Detailed source status, retrieval method and inaccessible documents are retained in `event_sources.json`.

The [UNESCO overview](https://ihp-wins.unesco.org/rapid-response/rasuwa-flash-flood-in-nepal) also distinguishes an earlier 8 July 2025 event. It was not substituted for the requested study. A corresponding NDRRMA PDF was discovered in search but not independently opened; no detailed measurements from that document are used.
