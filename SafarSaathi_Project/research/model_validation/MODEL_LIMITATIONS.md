# Model limitations

- Known holdouts are reused for audit; new horizon/baseline comparisons are exploratory, not a new independent trial.
- Annual history ends in 2019 and has five original test targets; no contemporary national or local demand validity follows.
- Monthly persistence is horizon-sensitive and weak at longer horizons. National FTA excludes NRI and cannot be interpreted as destination occupancy.
- Cyclone archive coverage and basin counts are not local hazard probabilities; annual PM2.5 is national modeled exposure, not a calibrated sensor forecast.
- NLP labels are source-domain binary sentiment; entity/time metadata and verified local community labels are absent. The selected Aditya1010 source manifest records Apache-2.0; do not generalize other corpus licenses to it.
- Flood labels combine permanent water and flood extent, and test pixels are spatially dependent. Four country holdouts do not validate Nepal or Uttarakhand transfer.
- Residual quantiles are descriptive, not calibrated prediction intervals. Test coverage, if recorded for a validation-residual band, is explicitly exploratory with small samples. No operational confidence percentage is supplied.
- No model is certified for emergency dispatch, casualties, road closure or evacuation decisions.
